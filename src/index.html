MAZE ROYALE

25 players. 25 mazes. One survivor.

  • You start in your home maze with 3 lives. Lose them all and your maze collapses.
  • Walk through the doors on any edge into neighboring mazes — the world wraps around. Side tunnels warp within a maze.
  • Eat ghosts away from home to send raiders into all four neighboring mazes. Power pellets freeze raiders; fruit wipes them out.
  • Clear a maze to spawn its fruit. Eating it refills the maze and raises its level. Raid a rival's fruit to reload your home power pellets.
  • Pellets charge your power-up (double away from home): Blackout, Frenzy or Daze. Grab airdrops too.
